# Lunatory Commercial Licensing

Lunatory is proprietary software intended to support practical inventory workflows while allowing the project owner to retain control of the software and its commercial rights.

## Evaluation vs. Production Use

Public availability of Lunatory documentation or compiled installers does not automatically authorize production business use.

Evaluation use may include:

- personal testing
- demonstration
- development testing
- limited non-production business evaluation

Production use means using Lunatory as part of the regular operations of a business, franchise, restaurant, organization, or other commercial entity.

Production use requires separate written authorization.

## Potential License Types

Commercial arrangements may be offered for:

- Single-location deployment
- Multi-location deployment
- Franchisee deployment
- Restaurant-group deployment
- Self-hosted deployment
- Custom integration
- Custom development
- Support and maintenance
- Extended update access
- Source-code access where separately negotiated

Exact pricing, permitted devices, permitted locations, support terms, update terms, and deployment rights may vary by agreement.

## Location-Specific Permission

Permission granted to one location does not automatically extend to:

- another store
- a franchise owner’s other locations
- a parent company
- a franchisor
- an affiliate
- a sister company
- another department or business unit

Any broader deployment requires separate written authorization unless the applicable license expressly says otherwise.

## No Transfer of Ownership

A commercial license grants only the rights written in that agreement.

Unless a separate written agreement expressly transfers ownership, commercial licensing does not transfer ownership of Lunatory, its original branding, proprietary source code, private build systems, or other protected project materials.

## Distribution and Resale

A standard commercial-use license does not automatically permit:

- resale
- sublicensing
- redistribution
- white-labeling
- rebranding
- bundling Lunatory into another commercial product
- providing copies to unrelated businesses

These rights require separate written permission.

## Source Code

The production source code is maintained privately.

Source-code access is not included in ordinary evaluation or production-use permission unless a separate written agreement explicitly grants it.

## Support and Availability

Unless specifically included in a commercial agreement, no guaranteed response time, uptime, support period, update schedule, or service-level agreement is provided.

## Third-Party Components

Lunatory may include third-party components governed by their own licenses. Those licenses continue to apply independently of any Lunatory commercial agreement.

## Licensing Contact

For commercial licensing, deployment discussions, integrations, or custom development, contact the Lunatory project owner through the official GitHub repository.
