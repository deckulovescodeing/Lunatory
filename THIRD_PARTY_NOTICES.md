# Third-Party Notices

Lunatory may incorporate third-party software, libraries, frameworks, models, fonts, icons, media, or other components.

Those components remain subject to their own copyright notices and license terms.

## Current Status

A complete dependency-by-dependency license audit should be performed before broad commercial distribution.

Until that audit is complete, this file serves as the public notice that third-party rights are not replaced or overridden by the Lunatory proprietary license.

## Requirements for Future Releases

Before publishing a production release, the private development repository should maintain an inventory of third-party components that includes, where applicable:

- package/component name
- version
- upstream project
- license identifier or license text
- required attribution
- redistribution conditions
- source-offer or notice obligations, if any
- whether the component is bundled in the distributed binary

## No Override of Third-Party Rights

Nothing in the Lunatory proprietary license restricts rights that a third-party license grants directly to users.

Likewise, nothing in the Lunatory proprietary license removes obligations imposed by a valid third-party license.

## AI and Models

Any bundled or externally used AI model/runtime should be reviewed separately for:

- model license
- commercial-use permissions
- redistribution permissions
- attribution requirements
- acceptable-use restrictions
- whether model weights are distributed with Lunatory

## Reporting a Missing Notice

If you believe Lunatory is missing a required third-party attribution or notice, contact the project owner through this repository with the component name and source.
